# Fallback and Approval Review

- Fallback disclosure: intended path, limitation, incomplete status, alternative,
  subject change, trade-off, and approval requirement are mandatory.
- Effect approval and execution-subject fallback approval are independent.
- Commit approval does not imply Push approval.
- Commit or Push approval does not imply Codex fallback approval.
- Manual Codex transfer is `NOT_DIRECT_CHATGPT_EXECUTION`.
- Alternatives must be ranked; no workaround is forced when none is equivalent.
- Mandatory response structure is included in the canonical standard.
